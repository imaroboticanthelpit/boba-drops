Knock knock! It's me! The Ratboy Genius!

Ratboy Genius is an animated YouTube webseries created by Ryan Dorin, which follows the adventures of Ratboy Genius, the ruler of Ratboy's Kingdom, and all of his many friends and adversaries. The series began in 2007 as a 2D animated series, but quickly transitioned to 3D CGI animation. Ryan Dorin creates all the original music the series is known for, and the series' soundtrack is all available for purchase on Bandcamp.
